Rose fed a table of eleven every Sunday and never once wrote anything down. Marcus spent a winter standing next to her at the stove with a notepad, measuring her handfuls in cups.

“Don’t squeeze them. You’re making meatballs, not a snowball fight.”Nana Rose, to anyone who touched her mixing bowl

Recipe

Sunday Meatballs

Serves 6Prep 30 minCook 3 hrs

For the meatballs

For the sauce

How to make them

  1. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the meats, eggs, cheese, garlic, parsley, salt and pepper. Mix with your hands until just combined, then roll into golf-ball-sized meatballs.
  3. Brown the meatballs in olive oil in a wide pot, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Set them on a plate.
  4. In the same pot, cook the onion until soft. Add the garlic for 30 seconds, then the tomato paste for 1 minute.
  5. Pour in the tomatoes and 1 cup of water. Add the oregano and a pinch of salt, and bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Return the meatballs to the pot. Cover partly and simmer on low for 2½ hours, stirring gently now and then.
  7. Tear in the basil. Serve over spaghetti or on crusty bread, with extra cheese.

Nana's note: make the sauce the day before if you can. It’s better on Monday.
